For many self-hosters, consultancies, and internal-tools users, AGPL-3.0 is fine. If that describes you, you can stop reading.

## When you may need a commercial licence

A paid commercial licence is available if any of the following apply:

- You want to **embed CSMP Risk Manager inside a closed-source product** that you distribute or offer as a SaaS.
- Your organisation's policies prohibit AGPL-3.0 in production environments.
- You are a managed-service provider (MSP/MSSP) selling a white-labelled risk-management service built on this codebase and cannot release your modifications.
- You need contractual warranties, indemnification, a defined SLA for security fixes, or long-term-support releases beyond what the open-source project provides.
- You want priority engineering support, onboarding, or custom integrations (SSO/SAML, SCIM, custom compliance frameworks, data-migration from legacy risk registers).

The commercial licence grants the same software under different terms — no AGPL copyleft obligations, with a formal contract and optional support package.
